Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about setting up WhatsApp automation for your business.

Can messages be sent with our store's verified name instead of a random number?
Yes! EasyToReach connects directly to official Meta WhatsApp Cloud API. Once verified, your business's official name and verified checkmark appear at the top of customer chats.
How does the automated birthday discount work?
Every day at 9:00 AM, our background engine scans your customer database. Anyone having a birthday today receives an exclusive personalized discount voucher (e.g. 15% OFF valid for 7 days). Our anti-spam ledger ensures no customer ever receives duplicate wishes in the same calendar year.
Can we import our existing customer Excel or Google Sheets list?
Yes. You can upload an Excel CSV file in 1 click or paste your Google Sheets link. EasyToReach will automatically pull names, mobile numbers, birthdays, and customer preferences without manual typing.
Is our customer data secure?
Absolutely. All customer data is stored in your private, isolated database. It is never sold, scraped, or shared with third-party advertising brokers.
